Our Story

Since 2014

A Family Notebook Turned Restaurant

Recipe Secrets started in a small Rivermill kitchen, built around a single, well-worn notebook of handwritten recipes passed down through three generations of the Marchetti family. What began as Sunday dinners for friends grew into a neighborhood favorite — and eventually, into the dining room you're sitting in today.

We still cook from that same notebook. Every dish carries a little bit of family history, updated with modern technique and the best ingredients Rivermill has to offer.

Meet the Chef

Chef Elena Marchetti

Elena grew up in that Rivermill kitchen, learning to cook at her grandmother's elbow before training at culinary school and working in kitchens across Italy and the Pacific Northwest. In 2014, she came home to open Recipe Secrets — a place to honor her family's recipes while giving them room to grow.

"Every plate we send out is still a little bit of my grandmother's kitchen," Elena says. "I just get to add my own secret ingredient."
